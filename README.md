## What I do

I design and run about 20 AI assistant agents on the Grok Bot platform for my businesses:

- Contract hunting on SAM.gov and local city bid portals
- Running my Shopify and Sticker Mule shops (listings, orders, profile upkeep)

Every agent works behind a human-approval gate: agents research, draft, and alert, and nothing gets sent, posted, or bought until I approve it.
